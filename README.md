# Child Mortality in a Changing World

> A data-driven analysis of global child mortality trends and selected socioeconomic and health-related determinants.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Child mortality remains a vital indicator of population health, socioeconomic development, and access to essential healthcare services. 

This project began as a broad exploration of global child mortality to examine long-term mortality trends and explore relationships between child mortality and several contextual indicators. The initial iteration incorporated datasets covering:
* Child mortality
* GDP per capita
* Population
* Health expenditure
* Water and sanitation
* Education
* Income group
* World Bank regions
* Country geolocation

### Project Evolution & Scope Pivot
The first dashboard produced a broad analysis covering **1980–2011**. However, a deeper audit of the underlying datasets revealed substantial inconsistencies in country and year coverage across variables. Rather than building on an uncertain analytical foundation, the project underwent a rigorous **country-year completeness assessment**. 

Variables with insufficient coverage were removed, the scope was redefined, and the dataset was rebuilt from the ground up. The final, revised analysis focuses on **2000–2024** across **212 countries** and evaluates child mortality alongside:
* GDP per capita
* Health expenditure
* Population
* Water and sanitation access

## 🎯 Research Question

> **"How has child mortality changed across countries over time, and what socioeconomic and health-related factors are associated with differences in child mortality?"**

*Note: This project is intentionally exploratory and descriptive. It does not attempt to establish causal relationships, but rather examines global trends, regional disparities, contextual associations, and data quality/completeness.*

---

## 🧭 Project Objectives

1. **Understand the Trend:** Analyze global changes in child mortality over the selected timeframe.
2. **Identify Disparities:** Highlight differences across countries and World Bank regions.
3. **Examine Potential Determinants:** Investigate associations with GDP per capita, health expenditure, population, and water/sanitation access.
4. **Assess Data Quality:** Systematically audit missing country-year observations before dataset integration.
5. **Build an Integrated Model:** Design a robust country-year star-schema analytical model in Power BI.
6. **Communicate Findings:** Deliver insights through an interactive dashboard and public data storytelling.
